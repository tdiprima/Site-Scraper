### Requirements

* GPA: 2.5+ (varies by major)
* 60+ transferable credits
* Major prerequisites
* Official transcripts and credit evaluations
* Deadlines:

  * Fall: March 1 (priority), June 1 (final)
  * Spring: November 1
  * Summer: April 1

## Housing Application Process

### Step-by-Step

1. Get accepted & receive NetID
2. Apply for housing in February
3. Roommate matching (after submission)
4. Assignments sent out in April-May
5. Move-in (late August)

### Key Deadlines

* Housing Application: Mid-March
* Fee: \$50 due at submission
* Move-In: Late August / Early September
